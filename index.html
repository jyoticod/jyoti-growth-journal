My emotional toolkit

Open this page whenever you feel overwhelmed. These exercises are meant to be practiced slowly.

🌿 STOP — when anger rises

Stop speaking for a moment.
Take a slow, comfortable breath.
Observe: “Am I hurt, scared, frustrated, or angry?”
Proceed calmly, or ask for a break.

“Mane atyare gusse aave che. Hu thodi vaar pachi shantithi vaat karish.”

🧠 Thought check — when I overthink

Write the thought. Ask: What are the facts? What am I assuming? What evidence points another way? What is a fairer thought?

Instead of “Everything was my fault,” try “I can take responsibility for my actions without assuming I caused everything.”

💗 Self-compassion with responsibility

“Hu mari bhulo mathi shikhi rahi chu. Hu mari responsibility laish, pan mari jat ne roj saja nahi aapu.”

Reflect on one repair you can make and one behavior you can practice differently.

🚶 Physical grounding

Try 10–20 minutes of comfortable walking, 5 minutes of gentle stretching, and regular sleep times. Choose joint-friendly movement and rest during pain or fatigue; avoid pushing through a flare.

🫶 When I miss someone

“I can miss someone and still care for myself today.” Name the feeling, place your feet on the ground, look around, and choose one small caring action.
